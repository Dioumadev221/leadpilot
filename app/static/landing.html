The problem

Inbound is where deals are won or lost

Forms, Upwork, LinkedIn and referrals all land in the same inbox. Someone has to read each one, look up the company, decide, copy it into the CRM and reply.

⏱

Hot leads wait

The best enquiry of the week sits behind newsletters and vendor pitches until someone finds time to triage. By then the buyer is talking to someone else.

🧹

The CRM fills with noise

SEO offers, outsourcing agencies and test submissions get copied in next to real buyers, and every report built on that data is wrong.

🎲

Nobody can explain the score

An LLM that says "82/100" can say 64 tomorrow for the same lead. Sales stops trusting the number and goes back to gut feeling.

How it works

A bounded pipeline, not a chatbot

Every lead takes the same five steps. No open-ended agent loops, so cost per lead is predictable and behaviour is testable.

Capture

Webhook for any form tool, REST API, or manual entry. Duplicate submissions are dropped with idempotency keys.

Enrich

Business, free or disposable email. Company website title and description, fetched safely.

Qualify

An AI agent extracts intent, ICP fit, budget, timeline and use case as structured data, quoting the lead.

Score

A rule table turns those signals into a 0-100 score with a per-point breakdown. Spam stops here.

Approve & sync

A writer agent drafts the CRM note and reply. You edit, approve, and the contact lands in your CRM.

Built for control

The AI reads. The rules decide. You approve.

LeadPilot uses the model for what it is good at, understanding messy text, and keeps everything that must be consistent out of its hands.

  • Deterministic scoring. Same lead, same score, every time. Each point is explained, and the thresholds are yours to tune.
  • Human in the loop. Nothing is written to the CRM until someone approves it, and the draft can be edited first.
  • Full audit trail. Every agent step, model or rules mode, duration and human decision is logged per lead.
  • Graceful degradation. If the model provider fails, the pipeline falls back to rules instead of stopping.

Reporting

The weekly pipeline report writes itself

Every Monday, an analyst agent turns the week into a short brief: volume, quality, sources, what buyers ask for, which hot leads still wait, and three actions for the week ahead.

  • Numbers from SQL, never from the model. The agent only writes the narrative around metrics it is given, so it cannot invent a figure.
  • Delivered where you work. In the console and in Slack.

Under the hood

Engineered like infrastructure

Python, FastAPI, LangGraph and PostgreSQL. Runs as a container or serverless, in your cloud, on your database.

Bounded LangGraph DAG

Four agent steps at most, with conditional routing. Two model calls per lead, so cost is known before you launch.

Structured outputs

Every agent returns a typed schema. The pipeline never parses free text from a model.

Postgres job queue

Atomic claims, retries with a cap, failed state with the error kept for inspection.

Pluggable CRM adapters

HubSpot and Zoho included, with retries on rate limits. A new CRM is one adapter file.

Safe enrichment

Website lookups only reach public addresses, with redirects checked hop by hop.

Tested in CI

Pipeline routing, scoring, CRM request shapes and the full approval flow, on SQLite and PostgreSQL.

FAQ

Questions teams ask first

Does the AI write directly into our CRM?

No. The agents qualify, score and draft. A person approves each lead, optionally after editing the note or reply, and only then is the contact created or updated in HubSpot or Zoho.

Can it follow our own definition of a good lead?

Yes. Your ideal customer profile is written in plain language for the qualifier agent, and the scoring rules and hot/warm thresholds are configuration, not prompts.

Which models does it use, and what does it cost to run?

OpenAI, Anthropic, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint such as Groq, Gemini or a self-hosted model. Each lead costs two model calls; with a small model that is a fraction of a cent per lead.

Where does our data live?

In your own PostgreSQL database and your own cloud account. LeadPilot ships as a Docker image and also runs serverless.

What if the model provider is down?

The qualifier and writer fall back to deterministic rules and templates, the lead is flagged with a warning, and nothing is lost.

Is the live demo real?

The pipeline is the production code. The workspace, Northwind Studio, is sample data, syncs to a sandbox CRM and resets every day. Send it a lead of your own and watch it go through.
